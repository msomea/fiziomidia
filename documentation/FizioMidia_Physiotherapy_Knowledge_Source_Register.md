# FizioMidia Physiotherapy Knowledge Source Register

**Document purpose:** Establish a curated, traceable and clinically
reviewed source register for the FizioMidia AI system, patient education
library and forum content agent.

**Project:** FizioMidia\
**Primary audience:** Tanzanian patients, caregivers, community members
and physiotherapy professionals\
**Knowledge languages:** English and Kiswahili\
**Document status:** Initial planning register --- sources and
individual documents require verification before production use\
**Version:** 1.0\
**Prepared:** 27 November 2026

------------------------------------------------------------------------

## 1. Purpose and scope

This register identifies authoritative sources from which FizioMidia may
obtain physiotherapy and rehabilitation information. It is intended to
support:

1.  The FizioMidia Physiotherapy Assistant, which provides general
    educational information and safe guidance without diagnosing or
    prescribing treatment.
2.  The Forum Content Agent, which prepares evidence-informed public
    education topics for administrator review.
3.  The FizioMidia Patient Education Library, which hosts original
    patient guides, leaflets and educational resources.
4.  The Virtual Administrator, which monitors knowledge-document review
    dates, source changes and content governance.

The register is a source-discovery and governance document. Inclusion of
an organization or URL does **not** mean that every item on that website
is clinically approved, current, suitable for Tanzanian patients, or
legally reusable.

## 2. Knowledge-source principles

FizioMidia should apply the following principles:

-   **Authority:** Prefer national health authorities, WHO, recognized
    professional bodies, evidence-synthesis organizations and
    established clinical guideline developers.
-   **Relevance:** Prioritize rehabilitation and physiotherapy
    information that can be safely adapted to the Tanzanian context.
-   **Currency:** Record publication and update dates. Check for newer
    editions, corrections or superseding guidelines.
-   **Evidence quality:** Distinguish clinical guidelines, systematic
    reviews, primary research, patient leaflets and general educational
    pages.
-   **Local applicability:** Do not automatically transfer
    recommendations from another country into Tanzania. Consider service
    availability, referral pathways, equipment, language, culture and
    local policy.
-   **Patient readability:** Prefer clear, practical information
    suitable for non-specialists.
-   **Bilingual quality:** Use professionally reviewed Kiswahili, not
    unreviewed machine translation.
-   **Traceability:** Every knowledge item must retain its source, exact
    title, URL, access date, version and review history.
-   **Copyright compliance:** Store or reproduce full documents only
    when the licence or written permission allows it. Otherwise retain
    permitted metadata, links and appropriately limited notes.
-   **Clinical review:** Only approved material may be used as
    authoritative grounding for public-facing AI answers.

## 7. Clinical review and approval policy

Use the following workflow for every source or original FizioMidia
educational document.

1.  **Discovery:** Identify the document and its issuing organization.
2.  **Authenticity check:** Confirm that the URL is official or that the
    document is hosted by a credible publisher or repository.
3.  **Currency check:** Review publication date, edition, update history
    and superseding guidance.
4.  **Evidence appraisal:** Identify whether it is a guideline,
    systematic review, primary study or general patient resource.
5.  **Local applicability:** Assess whether recommendations are
    appropriate for Tanzanian patients and services.
6.  **Copyright review:** Record licence terms and any restrictions on
    storage, translation, adaptation, display and commercial use.
7.  **Clinical review:** Obtain review by a suitably qualified
    physiotherapist or other relevant healthcare professional.
8.  **Language review:** Have Kiswahili content checked by a competent
    health-language reviewer.
9.  **Approval:** Record who approved the material and for which uses.
10. **Monitoring:** Recheck important sources periodically and whenever
    a source announces an update.

## 8. Copyright, licensing and reuse rules

FizioMidia must not assume that a publicly accessible PDF or patient
leaflet is free to copy, translate, store, adapt or redistribute.

Apply these rules:

-   Record the exact licence for each item, including its version and
    any conditions.
-   Check whether commercial use is permitted. This matters if
    FizioMidia has sponsored placements or other commercial activities.
-   Check whether translations and adaptations are permitted.
-   Preserve required attribution and notices.
-   Follow share-alike requirements where applicable.
-   Do not upload or redistribute full copyrighted documents without
    permission.
-   Where reuse is not permitted, store bibliographic metadata, a link
    and original FizioMidia notes within the limits of applicable law
    and terms.
-   Seek written permission when the licence is unclear.
-   Keep a record of permissions and correspondence.

Some WHO publications use Creative Commons
Attribution-NonCommercial-ShareAlike 3.0 IGO licensing. This is not
blanket permission for commercial reuse. Check the licence on the
individual publication and obtain appropriate advice before adapting it
for a commercial platform.

Official licence reference:
https://creativecommons.org/licenses/by-nc-sa/3.0/igo/

## 9. Rules for AI use of the knowledge library

### Physiotherapy Assistant

The assistant may:

-   Explain approved educational material in accessible English or
    Kiswahili.
-   Summarize relevant guidance in original wording.
-   Explain general rehabilitation principles.
-   Identify when professional assessment is appropriate.
-   Provide source titles and links where available.
-   Explain uncertainty and differences between sources.

The assistant must not:

-   Diagnose a user's condition.
-   Prescribe medication.
-   Present general education as an individualized treatment plan.
-   Claim that a particular intervention is guaranteed to cure a
    condition.
-   Invent clinical recommendations or citations.
-   Treat an unreviewed source as approved clinical guidance.
-   Delay emergency care by continuing an ordinary educational
    conversation.

### Forum Content Agent

The agent may:

-   Draft original educational topics using approved sources.
-   Suggest discussion questions and public-awareness topics.
-   Provide references and source links for administrator verification.
-   Identify gaps in the knowledge library.

The agent must:

-   Check for duplicate or substantially similar topics.
-   Avoid unsupported claims and fabricated references.
-   Flag conflicting or uncertain evidence.
-   Submit content for human administrator review.
-   Never publish a health-related topic before approval.

### Virtual Administrator

The Virtual Administrator may:

-   Monitor document status and review dates.
-   Identify documents that may be outdated.
-   Report missing source metadata.
-   Flag documents with unresolved licensing or clinical review.
-   Produce knowledge-library audit reports.

It must remain read-only and must not independently approve or alter
clinical knowledge documents.

## 10. Retrieval and technical implementation recommendation

For the FizioMidia MERN stack, implement Retrieval-Augmented Generation
(RAG).

Suggested pipeline:

1.  Store source metadata and permitted document content in MongoDB.
2.  Extract text from approved PDFs and web pages.
3.  Split text into meaningful sections while retaining page numbers,
    headings and source identifiers.
4.  Generate embeddings using an appropriately selected embedding model.
5.  Store vectors and metadata in MongoDB Atlas Vector Search, subject
    to the selected Atlas tier's feature and resource availability.
6.  Retrieve relevant approved sections for each user question.
7.  Pass only relevant, permitted excerpts to Gemini.
8.  Require answers to distinguish source-supported information from
    general explanation.
9.  Return source titles and links where the retrieved material supports
    the answer.
10. Log retrieval references and model output for quality monitoring,
    while minimizing personal data.

Do not assume that the Gemini model used for text generation is also the
appropriate embedding model. Select and test an embedding model
separately.

MongoDB Atlas Vector Search documentation:\
https://www.mongodb.com/products/platform/atlas-vector-search

Google Gemini API documentation:\
https://ai.google.dev/gemini-api/docs

## 14. Recommended review schedule

  -----------------------------------------------------------------------
  Material                            Suggested review interval
  ----------------------------------- -----------------------------------
  Emergency and red-flag guidance     At least every 6 months and
                                      whenever guidance changes

  Clinical guidelines                 Annually and whenever the issuing
                                      body updates them

  Patient education guides            Annually or when supporting
                                      guidance changes

  Research summaries                  When significant new evidence is
                                      identified

  National policies and strategies    Whenever a new edition or successor
                                      is published

  Copyright and permission records    Before each new use or adaptation
                                      if terms are unclear
  -----------------------------------------------------------------------

These are FizioMidia governance targets, not official review intervals
imposed by the source organizations.
